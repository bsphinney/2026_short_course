# Four-year short-course analysis figures (2023–2026)

Generated from the DIA-NN 2.6.0 re-search of all four short-course cohorts
(373 runs; beads / urea / S-Trap; contaminants excluded).

| Figure | Shows |
|---|---|
| `fig_pca_prep_vs_year.png` | Data clusters by **preparation** (PC2, 71.4% of that component) and by **student**, not by year |
| `fig_cv_by_prep_year.png` | Reproducibility per preparation per cohort — S-Trap most consistent (16.5–17.7%), beads most variable (19.4–39.5%) |
| `fig_success_rate.png` | Depth and success rate over time — beads is the preparation that fails (56–97%) |
| `fig_protein_classes.png` | S-Trap recovers the most transmembrane protein |
