Proteomics Short Course 2026 - student files
============================================

START HERE
----------
  HANDOUT-hive-start-here.html   The whole course. Open it in a browser.
  HANDOUT-hive-start-here.md     Same document, for reading on GitHub.

It comes down to two commands:

    ssh proteomics-class-NN@hive.hpc.ucdavis.edu     (NN is your own number)
    claude-course

Then ask for what you want in plain English - and always say
"the species is mouse".


GETTING YOUR RESULTS BACK
-------------------------
  Get My Results.cmd     <- double-click this one
  Get-My-Results.ps1     <- ASK YOUR INSTRUCTOR, it is not published

Both files must sit in the SAME folder. Double-click the .cmd, never the
.ps1 - Windows blocks downloaded .ps1 files, which is what the .cmd is for.

Get-My-Results.ps1 contains every class password, so it is handed out
privately instead of being put anywhere the class can read. If you cannot
find it here, that is on purpose.


GETTING THE RAW DATA (only if you really need it)
-------------------------------------------------
  Get My Raw Data.cmd    <- double-click this one
  Get-My-Raw-Data.ps1    <- keep it in the same folder

Asks for your username and initials, shows you how many runs matched and
how big they are, and waits for you to say yes.

  *** These are BIG. One run is about 2.2 GB. Your own four runs are
      about 8 GB. A whole bench group is 60-90 GB. The full dataset is
      235 GB. On classroom wi-fi, with everyone copying at once, this is
      slow for everybody.

      You do NOT need the raw files to do the course - Claude searches
      them where they already are on Hive, which is much faster. Copy
      them only to open a run in Bruker software on your own PC, and
      take your own runs rather than a whole bench. ***


STUCK?
------
The last section of the handout lists what usually goes wrong. After that,
ask Claude - explaining errors is one of the things it is best at.
