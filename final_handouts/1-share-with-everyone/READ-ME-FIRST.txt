Proteomics Short Course 2026 — student files
============================================

In this folder:

  HANDOUT-hive-start-here.html   Start here. Open it in a browser.
  Get My Results.cmd             Downloads your results to your own computer.

MISSING A FILE?
---------------
Get My Results.cmd needs a partner file called Get-My-Results.ps1, and that one
is NOT in this folder on purpose.

It contains the class account passwords, and everyone on the course can read
this shared folder — so it is handed out privately instead, the same way you
received your own password.

Ask your instructor for Get-My-Results.ps1. Save it in the SAME folder as
Get My Results.cmd, then double-click the .cmd file.

(The copy in superseded/ is an older version that asks you to type your password
every time. Do not use it.)
